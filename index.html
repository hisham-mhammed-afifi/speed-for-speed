HEATLINE

Open-city police pursuit. Outrun, outsmart and wreck the law.

How to play

Cruise the city. Speed past a patrol car, ram a cop, or press H to start a pursuit. Survive, wreck cops for bounty, then break line of sight and stay hidden to escape. Stay slow near cops and you get busted.

  • Heat rises the longer you run and the more cops you wreck: roadblocks at 3, spike strips at 4, a helicopter at 5.
  • Brake before corners. Grass slows you down, and heavy hits damage your car.
  • Phone: drag your left thumb to steer; right thumb has GAS, BRAKE (hold to reverse), DRIFT and NITRO. The top-left buttons pause and reset your car.
  • Gamepad: left stick steer, triggers throttle/brake, A handbrake, X nitro.
